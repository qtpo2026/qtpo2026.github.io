items:
  - q: "What is QTPO 2026?"
    a: |
      Quantum Technologies for Precision Oncology: From Drug Discovery to
      Radiation Therapy is an international workshop bringing together
      researchers, clinicians, engineers, technology developers, industry
      leaders, and decision-makers to explore the role of quantum and
      quantum-inspired technologies in cancer research and care, with a
      particular emphasis on radiation therapy and treatment planning.
  - q: "When and where is it?"
    a: |
      **Thursday, November 5 – Friday, November 6, 2026**, at Carolina at
      The Capitol, 101 Constitution Avenue NW, Washington, DC. See the
      [Program](/program) page for the day-by-day structure.
  - q: "Is the workshop in person or online?"
    a: |
      Both — QTPO 2026 is a hybrid event. Day 1 will be available in
      person and online; Day 2 is primarily an in-person track for
      industry and government participants.
  - q: "Who should attend?"
    a: |
      Physicians, medical physicists, quantum researchers and engineers,
      industry representatives, and government and funding-agency
      stakeholders working at the intersection of quantum computing and
      oncology. Students and early-career researchers are welcome.
  - q: "How do I register?"
    a: |
      QTPO 2026 does not have open registration — speakers are invited
      directly by the organizing committee. If you're interested in
      attending as a guest, use the "I'm Interested in Attending" link on
      the [Speakers](/speakers) page (or in the site header, once it's
      live).
  - q: "How can I propose a talk?"
    a: |
      Speakers are invited directly by the organizing committee rather
      than through an open call for proposals. If you'd like to be
      considered, reach out to one of the organizers listed on the
      [Organizers](/organizers) page.
  - q: "Who is organizing and sponsoring QTPO 2026?"
    a: |
      See the [Organizers](/organizers) and [Sponsors](/sponsors) pages.
  - q: "Who do I contact with questions?"
    a: |
      Reach out to the lead organizers listed on the
      [Organizers](/organizers) page.
